1. Program Portfolio

Zone01 Kisumu

Program Type: Flagship Talent Accelerator

Duration: 12 months

Location: Kisumu, Kenya

LakeHub's flagship talent accelerator. It offers a fully funded, peer-to-peer, and gamified 1-year software engineering curriculum that prepares local youth for enterprise roles in the digital economy.

Key Features:

Fully funded program — no cost to participants 

Peer-to-peer learning model 

Gamified curriculum for engagement 

Production-ready project portfolio 

Direct connections to 45+ hiring partners 

Outcomes:

88% direct job placement rate within 6 months of graduation 

500+ alumni across global tech ecosystems 

12+ months of intensive, hands-on portfolio building
